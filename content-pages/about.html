i'm garett, a programmer and hobbyist game developer from southern california. i've been fascinated by computers and games my whole life, so much so that i spent four years getting a degree in computer science at UC Riverside (class of 2025!!). currently, i'm working towards a masters degree in computer science (also at UC Riverside), which i only have a few months left for!

during my undergrad, i was heavily involved with the game dev club on campus, Gamespawn. i was the treasurer for one year and collaborated with other members on a whole bunch of games!

i don't have a lot of projects on here (yet), but i hope to keep it updated with pretty much every project i get to a showable state. some may be unfinished.

topics that interest me the most are computer graphics, game design, embedded systems, and education.

in my free time, i make, play, and think about games. it's mostly thinking these days. i also like music and cooking and stuff.
