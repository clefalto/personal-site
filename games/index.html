present your purrmit

VR comedy simulation game where you play as a border inspector for a fictional country populated entirely* by cats.

you're tasked with the incredibly difficult job of verifying potential entrants' information on their permits.

this project was made in a group of two other students as the final project for our VR class! development took place over about two and a half weeks. most 3d assets were obtained from asset packs online, but all 2d assets were drawn by a member of our team.
